BokyDo is a free, open-source task manager you host yourself. This app talks to your
server and no one else's: no accounts with third parties, no tracking, no Google services
required.

- Inbox, Today and Upcoming lists, projects, labels, filters and boards
- Natural-language quick add, parsed on the device
- Sub-tasks, comments, reminders that fire offline as exact alarms
- Voice capture (Ramble): speak a brain-dump, review the drafted tasks, commit
- Homescreen widgets, Quick Settings tile, share target and shortcuts
- Syncs over your connection; changes queue offline and send later

Your tasks live in your PostgreSQL database. Source code (AGPL-3.0) and release builds
are reproducible: anyone can rebuild this exact APK from the published source.
